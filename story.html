Our Story

OUR HERITAGE

A Story of Flavour, Tradition & Royalty

The Royal Spice began with my father's dream and years of hard work.He always want to make a restaurant not just for business but for the people to enjoy the authentic taste of India. Mr. Manoj Kumar Verma turned his vision into reality through dedication, perseverance, and a passion for serving people.

The Royal Spice was founded by Mr. Manoj Kumar Verma (Founder,My Father) , a hardworking man whose dedication and determination inspired this restaurant. With years of hard work and a dream of creating something of his own, he built this place with passion. Every dish we serve reflects his commitment, perseverance, and love for bringing people together through food.

The Royal Spice was inspired by India's rich and diverse culinary traditions — a heritage built over centuries of royal kitchens, family recipes, and regional flavours passed down through generations.

Every ingredient that enters our kitchen is carefully selected for its freshness and quality, because we believe a truly great dish begins long before it reaches the tandoor.

Our vision has always been simple: to create an authentic dining experience that transports every guest to the heart of India, without ever leaving their seat.

We stay true to traditional Indian cooking techniques — slow simmering, hand-ground spices, and time-honoured methods — while blending it all with a spirit of modern hospitality.

The result is a restaurant that honours the past while welcoming you, warmly, into the present.
