# 👑 The Royal Spice

A modern and elegant multi-page restaurant website designed and developed as a static business website project.

The website represents a fictional Indian restaurant called **The Royal Spice**, combining traditional Indian cuisine with a modern and royal visual design.

---

## 🌐 Live Website

Visit the live website:

[The Royal Spice Website](https://github.com/VermaCo08/The-Royal-Spice.git)

---

## ✨ Features

- Responsive design for desktop and mobile
- Multi-page website structure
- Interactive navigation
- Home page
- Restaurant menu
- Our Story page
- Food gallery
- Contact section
- Smooth scrolling and animations
- Hover effects and transitions
- Local image storage
- Mobile-friendly layout

---

## 📄 Pages

### 🏠 Home
Introduction to The Royal Spice, featured dishes, restaurant story, and main sections.

### 🍽️ Menu
Displays signature dishes and starters with prices and descriptions.

### 📖 Our Story
Shares the story behind the restaurant and the journey of its founder.

### 🖼️ Gallery
Displays food, restaurant interior, and hospitality images.
