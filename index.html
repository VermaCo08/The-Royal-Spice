Our Story

A TASTE OF ROYAL INDIA

The Royal Spice began with my father's dream and years of hard work. Mr. Manoj Kumar Verma turned his vision into reality through dedication, perseverance, and a passion for serving people.

The Royal Spice was born from a love of India's rich culinary heritage. Every recipe is passed down through generations, prepared with fine ingredients and cooked with patience — so each plate tells a story of tradition, warmth, and royalty.
